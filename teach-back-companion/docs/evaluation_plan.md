# Proposed evaluation (not conducted)

Nothing here has been done. Any study with patients or staff needs UW approval, an IRB
determination, and consent procedures. No results below are real.

## Stage 1: expert review (no patients)

- 3 to 5 reviewers (hospitalist, cardiology or HF nurse, pharmacist, patient-education
  specialist) complete `clinical_review_checklist.md` for all three plans.
- Outcomes: proportion of plain versions judged faithful; critical facts added or corrected;
  misconception patterns added.

## Stage 2: evaluator validation (no patients)

- Collect 50 to 100 rewrites of real (de-identified) or template discharge instructions:
  human simplifications, AI-generated versions, professional translations.
- Two clinicians independently label each for clinically important discrepancies.
- Outcomes: evaluator sensitivity and specificity for critical discrepancies against the
  clinician consensus; false-alarm burden per 100 instructions; inter-rater agreement.

## Stage 3: usability (simulated)

- Staff or volunteer participants use synthetic scenarios.
- Outcomes: task completion, System Usability Scale, time to complete teach-back, accuracy
  in identifying documented next steps before and after use.

## Stage 4: pilot (only with sponsorship and approvals)

- Possible design: nurse-led discharge teaching with versus without the companion for one
  instruction template, on a single unit.
- Outcomes: patient recall of critical facts at discharge and at a 48 to 72-hour call;
  misunderstandings identified before discharge; staff time; safety events; equity across
  health literacy and language groups.
