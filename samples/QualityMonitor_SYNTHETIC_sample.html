8. Limitations

  • Differences are descriptive. CMS publishes adjusted, whole-number percentages without standard errors, so no statistical significance is claimed or computed.
  • A gap of about 1 pp can be produced by rounding alone.
  • HCAHPS covers a sample of adult inpatients who returned a survey; it does not describe every patient or every service.
  • National values are averages over all reporting hospitals, which differ in size, case mix and mission from an academic medical center.
  • Results reflect a reporting window that ends months before publication.
  • Directionality comes from CMS measure-ID conventions; IDs outside those rules are shown but not interpreted.
  • Nothing here identifies causes or recommends clinical action.

9. Methodology

  • A hospital measure is comparable only when its value is numeric and within 0-100, exactly one valid national row exists with the same measure ID, both cover the identical reporting window, and the response category text matches.
  • Gap (pp) = UWMC % − national %. Favorable gap flips the sign for bottom-box measures.
  • Gap bands: large ≥ 5.0 pp, moderate ≥ 3.0 pp, otherwise small. These are reading aids, not significance thresholds.
  • Confidence: low with CMS footnote 6 or 10 or fewer than 100 completed surveys; caution with any other footnote or fewer than 300 surveys; otherwise standard. Weights {'standard': 1.0, 'caution': 0.75, 'low': 0.5}.
  • Signal score = |favorable gap| × confidence weight + 1.0 if, since a comparable prior release, the gap to national grew by more than 1 pp in the same direction. Only top-box measures are ranked, so complementary boxes of one question are not counted twice.
