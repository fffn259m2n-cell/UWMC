Review queue

review_queue.csv lists every record with its suggested priority and the reason. Fill in reviewer_decision (include, exclude or unsure) and reviewer_notes, then run ep-copilot import-reviews review_queue.csv and ep-copilot evaluate to measure screening precision and recall. Reviewer decisions are stored separately and never overwritten by the tool.

Methods & limitations

  • PubMed is searched by Entrez date (when a record entered PubMed), so older articles indexed this period are included; publication dates are shown separately.
  • Screening uses configured keyword rules over title, abstract, keywords and MeSH terms. Records are never dropped; out-of-scope ones are labelled.
  • Extraction reads abstracts only, keeps the verbatim sentence for every value, and leaves a field empty when it is not explicitly stated. It does not assess bias, quality or clinical significance.
  • Trial changes are detected by comparing stored snapshots field by field, not only the registry's last-update date. Tracked trials that drop out of the status filter are re-read individually.
  • Abstracts and registry text remain the property of their publishers and sponsors; outputs link to sources rather than republishing them.
  • Screening precision/recall and extraction accuracy are unmeasured until a person reviews a sample.
