UWMC Quality Signal Monitor

A reproducible report on UW Medical Center's publicly reported HCAHPS patient-experience results, led by discharge and care-transition measures, compared with matched national values after every CMS refresh.

What it does

  • Downloads the official CMS datasets (HCAHPS Hospital, HCAHPS National, Hospital General Information) and records URLs, dates and checksums.
  • Verifies facility 500008 by name, city and state before analysing anything.
  • Compares each measure with the national value only when the measure ID, reporting window and response category all match; otherwise it explains why not.
  • Leads with discharge information, medicines, care coordination and symptom information; accounts for the 2025 HCAHPS survey update.
  • Lists data issues: suppression, footnotes, small samples, schema changes.

What it does not do

  • No statistical significance claims (CMS publishes no standard errors).
  • No causes, quality judgements or clinical recommendations.
  • No patient-level data; no access to UW systems.

Status
