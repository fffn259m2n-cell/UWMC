EP Research Copilot

A monthly, one-command digest of new AF-ablation literature and registered trials, with evidence pulled from each abstract next to the exact sentence it came from.

What it does

  • Finds new PubMed articles for a saved, documented query (by PubMed entry date, so nothing indexed between runs is missed); removes duplicates.
  • Sorts them for review by topic: pulsed-field ablation, recurrence, monitoring, repeat ablation, safety. Nothing is discarded.
  • Extracts from abstracts: design terms, sample size, interventions, follow-up, outcome statements, effect estimates and p-values, trial IDs and limitations. Each comes with its source sentence.
  • Tracks ClinicalTrials.gov: what changed since last month (status, enrollment, outcomes, dates, results flag, Washington State sites).

What it does not do

  • No systematic review, appraisal or clinical recommendations.
  • No full text; abstracts only, labelled as such.
  • No patient or institutional data; no login; no paid services.

Status

  • 54 automated tests; CI on Python 3.11 to 3.13.
  • Sample run on real records: 26 PubMed articles (one week) and 396 tracked trials.
  • Screening precision and recall will be measured from reviewer decisions; it is not yet measured.
Try it: pip install -e . && ep-copilot run (about 1 minute), then open output/ep_research_digest.html. Monthly upkeep: about 30 minutes. New topics are a configuration edit.
